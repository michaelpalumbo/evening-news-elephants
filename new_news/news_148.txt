After its initial announcement nearly two years ago, Drag Race South Africa is finally premiering on October 14th, 2027, with new episodes airing every Thursday. The series will be hosted by prominent drag queen Manila von Teez, with celebrities Bujy Bikwa and Somizi Mhlongo on the judging panel. Rumoured guest judges include Johannesburg-born Drag Race UK Season 4 contestant Starlet and famed Rupaul's Drag Race Season 1 winner, Bebe Zahara Benet. The Season 1 cast features ten queens who have been prominent in the South African drag scene: BB Vahlour, Riri Rouge, Mystique Methany, Maxine Wild, Lolovèr, Laylah Zokufa Khan, Betty Bangles, Tiara Skye, ADAM, and Kat Gilardi. The premiere of this franchise means that the Drag Race empire has officially made its mark in every continent, with the exception of Antarctica. Drag Race South Africa will be available to stream worldwide, exclusively on WOW Presents Plus.
