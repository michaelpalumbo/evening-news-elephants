A24 has announced that all child characters in their newly announced horror film, tentatively titled, "Skin of Your Teeth" will be CGI. While most of the details around the movie are still ambiguous, the film production company has stated the movie will be too horrific to ethically cast real child actors. While some critics speculate that the decision will make it impossible to take the movie seriously, some applaud the decision as a step towards reducing child exploitation within the film industry.
