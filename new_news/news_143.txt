An earnest act of friendship or a subtle political critique? Controversy surrounds Thai Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul after his recent serenade of American vice president JD Vance. When, during a formal luncheon, the Prime Minister broken into an impromptu acapella cover of the Beatles' "Let It Be". Though Charnvirakul's musical prowess is well-known, having sang for a number of world leaders and even serving as saxophone accompaniment for Malaysian Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim's performance of Frank Sinatra's My Way, in this case, Charnvirakul's choice of material, when the United States is actively threatening ambitions in both South America and Greenland, has led some to wonder if the Prime Minister was trying to use art to deliver a message.
