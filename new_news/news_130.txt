The Ghana Space Science and Technology Institute (GSSTI) announces Pan African Moon Settlement by 2035. The GSSTI has publicly unveiled its plans for a modular moon settlement, including its planned collaborations with the space programs of Nigeria, Ethiopia, Algeria, and Kenya. The participating nations will each provide researchers for the settlement and construct elements of the moon habitat. The White House has raised concerns about the potential of Chinese, Russian, and Saudi funding behind the project, citing American national security risks should the settlement be established. The GSSTI denies these claims.
