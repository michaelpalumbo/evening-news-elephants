Canadian Health Minister Marjorie Michel is pushing for houselessness to be categorized as a mental illness in the next edition of the DSM. Michel argues that being unhoused completely matches the definition of mental disorder, which is currently described by the World Health Organization as "significant disturbance in an individual's cognition, emotional regulation, or behaviour." Both housing and mental health advocates decry this suggestion, calling it a blatant denial of the structural causes of houselessness. They also say that defining houselessness as an illness could set the precedent for other significant decisions to be enacted, such as the option to participate in MAID, if one is without a home.
