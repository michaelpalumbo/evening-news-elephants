A large piece of aerial debris has blocked the main logging road in Fairy Creek, making the area inaccessible to large vehicles and inhibiting the RCMP's ability to remove protestors from the area. Advocates for protecting the old growth forest claim that the current atmospheric instability makes the protection of these ancient trees even more important, saying that the trees are so large they reduce the impact of atmospheric fragments. While the RCMP denies this claim, reporters in the area say they have not witnessed any large fallen trees, despite the large amounts of aerial debris in the area.
