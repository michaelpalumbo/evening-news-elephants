The protest against the refusal of the Quebec Superior Court to overturn their government's ban on religious symbols reaches its fourth day. Protestors held joint religious services in the streets of Montreal and Quebec city, with Catholic mass, Lutheran Service, Muslim Prayer circles and Jewish Service all happening simultaneously in the streets. Outside of the religious demonstrations, many secular protestors danced in solidarity, wearing a multitude of different religious symbols simultaneously, despite it currently being illegal to do so in the province.
