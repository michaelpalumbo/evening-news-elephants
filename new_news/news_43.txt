A popular atmospheric debris prediction site, Where2Hide, has been officially debunked, proving to be part of a nefarious dark web scheme. Dark web users could pay a fee to have a location listed as a likely area to be hit by aerial debris, often leading to residents of the area evacuating. Police investigators in Boise, Idaho discovered the ruse, when a string of burglaries occurred in the town, at addresses previously listed on the site. The IP addresses of both the clients and web site host were collected, and the police currently have warrants out for both parties. This is your friendly reminder to only trust aerial debris warnings from trusted sources, like local government authorities, and check back with us regularly to find out whether recommendations are to evacuate, or shelter in place.
