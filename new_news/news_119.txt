The University of Toronto Sessional Instructors and Teaching Assistants move into their second month of strike, further disrupting Fall classes. Collective bargaining between the unit and the university broke down in the summer and both sides are reported as being some distance from returning to the bargaining table. CUPE 3902 president Marianna Reis stated that there "will be no return to work until the vital contributions of part-time faculty and teaching assistants are recognized with wages and benefits that meet the standards of urban life." The Provost's office for the university has declined to comment.
