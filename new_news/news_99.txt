Toronto is officially the first Canadian location for the popular seltzer cafe chain, Bublitz. The shop, which specializes in a wide range of unique botanical flavours of the sugar free sparkling beverage, opened its first location in Los Angeles in June, and quickly found virality for its "Wild bergamot and vanilla espresso martini" flavoured seltzer. The Toronto location is set to open in early November, at the corner of Shaw and Queen.
