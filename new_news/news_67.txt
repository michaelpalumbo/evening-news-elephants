Dolly Parton cuts the ribbon at the grand opening of the Dollywood Lending Library, a new addition to the country music star's theme park in Pigeon Forge, Tennessee. Parton, 81, has been a champion of children's literacy through her Imagination Library, and now wants to encourage reading as fun for the whole family. Guests enter via a waterslide and are blasted with full-body Dyson air dryers in order to protect the books from water damage.
