Award winning, New York based immersive theatre company PunchDrunk premieres their newest work, "Drive your Plow over the bones of the dead" next month. The play, an adaptation of Nobel Prize-winning author Olga Tokarczuk's book of the same name, takes place on a 12 acres estate 80 minutes North of New York City that has been transformed into a replica of the Polish countryside. Tickets are already sold out for the current run, but started at $432+taxes.
