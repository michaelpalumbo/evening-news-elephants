Eurovision has announced Israel will not be permitted to compete in the 2028 competition. While many are pushing for the competition to ban Israel, given the consistent documentation of war crimes within the country, Eurovision spokespersons say that it was their independent investigation of the 2026 voting influence scandal that led them to ban the country. After almost two years of investigation, the organization has found that Israel's multi million dollar marketing campaign to secure votes went against the rules of the competition, disqualifying them from future participation.
