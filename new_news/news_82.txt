A new, highly exclusive gym has opened in Toronto at Bay and Bloor. Those who have attempted to walk in and register have reported not only being turned away but actually being completely ignored by receptionist staff, as if they were not visible. Meanwhile, many city residents have taken to social media to post images of the 7 karat gold-embossed envelopes they have received, inviting them to join the gym. According to amateur internet sleuths, there is no clear pattern as to who receives the invitations. Those who have tried to enter with the invitation are acknowledged by receptionists and told it is a $5,000 a month membership fee. As far as we know, no one has yet entered the gym, or at least, if they have, they have not posted about it.
