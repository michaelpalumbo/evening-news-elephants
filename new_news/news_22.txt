Yesterday middle school students at Trinity Merton Secondary School protested the complete ban of social media for people under the age of 18 by throwing paper airplanes back and forth to each other during their third period classes. Student spokespersons said they were using the paper planes as "messages," citing historical examples of non violent protests such as Tiananmen Square and The 1963 March on Washington. Their teachers, who collected a few of these planes, said the notes written on them included simple messages such as "the government is unc" and "skibidi," as well as quote "crude images" such as penises and caricatures of the teachers.
