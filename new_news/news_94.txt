The Trump administration is yet again receiving widespread criticism for sending deportees to alternative countries besides their homelands. Reports of packed commercial flights headed towards Southern Guyana, which has generally been devoid of human occupation since concurrent fallings began in the area last May. Experts say the region receives approximately one impact a week, making the area incredibly dangerous. Beyond environmental hazards, the area has been without utilities, medical resources and stable food sources for months. The deportations have been called "executions from above" and have been widely condemned as inhumane and antidemocratic.
