The Ministry of Health in Russia has quietly begun subsidizing conversion therapy across the country. This decision follows Putin's order to Health Minister Mikhail Murashko to initiate research of LGBTQ+ social behaviours in the state-run Serbsky Psychiatric Center. The head of the center, Zurab Kekelidze, has openly stated his belief that homosexuality is a mental disorder, and therefore treatable with corrective therapeutic programming. Families can now send their children to conversion therapy programs for no cost, as it's now considered to be a part of standard health care.
