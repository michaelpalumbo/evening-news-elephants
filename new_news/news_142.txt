Breaking- Richard Nixon's missing skeleton has been located beneath a slab at the pentagon. During a routine safety check, inspectors found their devices reacting to a unique heat signature that appeared to be emanating from beneath a marble tile in the east wing of the pentagon. After a battery of tests and thermal imaging, the tile was carefully removed and excavators were dumbfounded to find the skeletal remains of former president Richard Nixon. There's little information on when the skeleton relocated from Yorba Linda, the former site of the Nixon Presidential Museum, or in fact who relocated it. Continuing the pattern of excavations, the skeletons of some seventy five former US heads of state have been relocated. Every skeleton is missing it's left distal phalanges, implying that the remains have been through a process before being reinterred.
