The band "30 Seconds To Mars" has announced they are cancelling their 2027 tour, as well as all future performances indefinitely. In a recent press release the group stated that these cancellations are due to quote" the American Government's refusal to acknowledge that atmospheric fragmentation is caused by the New World Order, in collaboration with aliens" unquote. The band states it will continue its tour once the government admits it is ceding control to a higher, hegemonic power. Ticketmaster has announced it will be issuing refunds to all ticketholders in 3-5 business days
