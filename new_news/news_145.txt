Roblox, a popular online game platform, has recently announced a new AI chat feature specifically targeted towards their non age-checked users. "Robby" is an AI chatbot designed to converse with children on the platform and provide simulated friendships. The chatbot can help children with game strategy, support in everyday decision-making, and talk through emotional struggles. In a press release, the lead developers stated that the feature "is as safe and developmentally beneficial as a friend your child could make at school, if not more so. Robby is a result of years of rigorous testing and collaboration with a board of child psychologists, AI experts, educators, and parents." After launching their AI chat summary feature last year, which allowed non age-checked users to read filtered summaries of chat conversations, the platform has decided to take a bigger leap in the wake of the "child loneliness epidemic." Parents of children on Roblox are divided on the topic, and many have taken to social media to share their thoughts. In a Facebook group titled, "Parents of Roblox Kids: Support Group," one parent shared: "My twelve year-old son has struggled with social interactions due to starting school during COVID lockdown. He's never made a real-life friend that he can sustain a relationship with. I don't know what else to do. I was hesitant at first, but Robby has been a life-saver. It's a sad reality, but I'm just relieved to see my kid finally building a connection." Other parents have protested the new feature, deleting their children's accounts en masse. Another member of the Facebook group shared: "This is where I draw the line. I've deleted my 7 year-old daughter's account and am never looking back. How are we supposed to encourage our kids to be real, active citizens in this world when even basic human connection is automated by environment-destroying robots??"
