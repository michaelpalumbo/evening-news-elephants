Hamilton residents occupy data centre building site. On the evening of October 2nd, five Hamilton residents established a protest camp on the proposed site of the green-lit Hamilton Data Centre. Ashley Munekian, the groups' media liaison commented, "The release of the water used for data centre cooling back into Lake Ontario will increase the water temperature and therefore toxic blue-green algae blooms, increasing the toxicity of the lake for swimmers and wildlife who call Lake Ontario home. It is our duty to protect these waters from short sightedness for the next seven generations of human and non human residents". Police have served the group a notice for trespassing and an order to vacate the site within 48 hours. Munekian reports the group has no intention of leaving the site.
