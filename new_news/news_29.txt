Elon Musk is feeling some heat from the stock market today from investors as the sell-off of shares from SpaceX continues. Investors are concerned about Starlink's profitability following disruptions to service from atmospheric fragmentation. A representative of the company, speaking under conditions of anonymity, suggested that the company is betting on Grok to solve the problem.
