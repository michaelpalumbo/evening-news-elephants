Drop shipping company Alibaba is in hot water after a piece of aerial debris crashed into one of their factories in Hangzhou (Hung-joe), China causing the collapse of the west side of the building and revealed the internal workings of the facility. Within the manufacturing facility, large amounts of CFCs and lead paint were being used to manufacture value novelty electronics, such as cosmetic mini fridges and decorative cat fountains. Experts are saying the building collapse, along with heavy winds, likely created the ideal conditions for these contaminants to spread throughout the city. Residents within a 20-block radius of the factory were urged to stay indoors with their windows closed. At least 6 workers were killed in the collapse, with the final casualty count still to be determined.
