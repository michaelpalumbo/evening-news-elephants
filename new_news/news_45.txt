Toronto Police Officers executed an early morning raid on the homes of the two suspected organizers of last month's violent protest at city hall, which was against the ceasing of the Toronto Air Show. TPD broke down doors at 5:15AM, arresting both suspects and confiscating laptops, cell phones, and various pamphlets and other "propagandizing literature." The suspects have not yet been named publicly, but are charged on accounts of inciting violence, violence against a police officer, and disrupting the peace.
