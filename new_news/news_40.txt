Despite months of international outcry, Amazon still refuses to ban the selling of "Safety Domes" on its site. Safety Domes first started popping up for sale online in March after a series of celebrity social media posts went viral, in which celebrities like Emma Chamberlain, Kendall Jenner and Jelly Roll were showing off newly built protective dome structures that encased their homes. These original Safety Domes were built by the now defunct company, Harbinger Inc., and were said to be able to protect against any falling atmospheric fragment of up to 25 tons. Knock-off companies began selling similar domes shortly thereafter, offering the same peace of mind, but at a fraction of the cost. Harbinger Inc.'s claim was quickly disproved on April 12 when a relatively small atmospheric fragment hit Jenner's 3.2 million dollar beach house, shattering it instantly upon impact. Since then, Safety Domes have all but disappeared for sale online until two weeks ago when a few started showing up again on Amazon and selling out in seconds.
