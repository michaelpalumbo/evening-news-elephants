The Canadian government is announcing a temporary pause to all Freedom of Information Claims from both journalistic and civilian organizations. When questioned on the decision, press secretary Rachel Bendayan (Ben-day-an) stated that their office could not respond to the volume of claims, given the current atmospheric crisis. When a journalist mentioned the possible infringement on the Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms, Secretary Bendayan reminded the press that the invocation of the Emergencies Act, which occurred last December, has not yet been lifted by the government, thereby allowing this restriction of transparency. Civil Rights organizations across the country have promised to sue on behalf of citizens, although experts state that under the Emergencies Act, there is little chance of victory.
