The Winnipeg District School Board has announced layoffs for 376 education assistant positions, most of whom worked with Kindergarten-aged students. Both the city's teachers' union and local parental groups are criticizing the decision, saying that the staffing shortages caused by the layoffs will likely end diagnostic programming in early elementary education. They say this would reduce the wellbeing of disabled students, as well as make it more difficult for educators to notice signs of neglect or abuse.
