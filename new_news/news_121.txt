The SIU is investigating two officers in Haileybury, northern Ontario, after a suspect was killed during a car chase. The chase, which occurred around 3:15 AM last night, began after the suspect's vehicle did not stop for a randomized check-point. Officers began pursuit, eventually reaching speeds above 150 km/hour. The chase ended when the suspect lost control of their vehicle, swerving into a lamp post. Haileybury Police Officers have reported they found no criminal activity associated with the driver, besides missing the late night check point.
