A stray cat has stolen the hearts of tourists in Wellington, New Zealand after repeatedly riding the cable car through the country's capital. The cat was nicknamed Bilbo by cable car staff for his habit of going "there and back again," much like the protagonist of Tolkien's 'The Hobbit.' Bilbo is very friendly to passengers, often hopping into their laps or onto their shoulders uninvited. Animal experts say it is unlikely he is a feral cat because of his comfortability with humans, but despite a citywide campaign, no previous owners of the cat could be located. Wherever Bilbo came from, he is spreading joy to everyone who rides the rails with him.
