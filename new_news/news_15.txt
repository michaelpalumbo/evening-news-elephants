Massachusetts saw its first verdict under the new set of laws governing family vlogging and kidfluencers online. Amanda Bairdy, a 19 year-old ex-kidfluencer, began a lawsuit against her parents last March, after the implementation of the new guidelines passed earlier that month. Bairdy claimed withheld financial assets, emotional duress and a lack of consent for her participation in content on the Bairdy Bird channel, which began when she was only 28 weeks old. The Bairdys continued to post vlogs and other content throughout her upbringing, from life updates and advice, to prank videos. The court ruled in Amanda's favour, citing an overwhelming amount of video evidence in which she dissented to being filmed by her parents over the years. Law experts claim this case indicates we may be seeing far more family vlogger-based lawsuits in the near future.
