An elementary school in Termez, Uzbekistan has been placed under strict quarantine following a case of mass hysteria. Initially reported as prolonged laughing fits, updates have confirmed additional symptoms of crying, shaking, yelling and erratic movements. The first indication of symptoms arose in a group of 2nd graders allegedly sharing a joke. However when the students could not cease laughing adults and classmates became concerned. By the time health authorities had arrived a significant percentage of the student body were in hysterics. There is currently no known treatment for mass hysteria.
