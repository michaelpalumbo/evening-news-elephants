Site development for a mine in Moosonee, part of the "Ring of Fire" was halted last week after a polar bear attempted to attack the vehicles of site surveyors. While no one was injured in the interactions, the bear caused significant damage to the outside of the surveyors vehicles, and no staff was willing to exit and complete their surveys. While seeing polar bears in the Southernmost part of James Bay, Ontario is uncommon, it is not unprecedented. The Government of Ontario's website lists Moosonee as within their seasonal migratory range. Metalia Unlimited has requested a one-time permit to kill the polar bears around the mine, citing worker safety. However, the Ontario Hunting and Fishing office has denied their request and is investigating non-lethal methods to discourage polar bears from traversing near the mine site.
