The Canadian Supreme Court has reached a verdict on a case of domestic violence where a Ring camera was used as a critical form of evidence. The Ring camera, which was placed inside the home of a neighbour, faced the accused's bedroom, capturing clear evidence of abuse. However, the court ruled this evidence inadmissible, as it breached privacy rights by non-consensually recording a private residence. The case was thrown out and the accused walked free. Although the court said in their verdict that they regretted the lack of consequences for the accused, the potential ramifications the precedent would set for privacy rights was too high to indict.
