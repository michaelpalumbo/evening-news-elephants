Misty the golden retriever/husky mix has made news by taking herself to doggie
daycare Friday morning. Misty’s owner had kept her home this past Friday because she
had a vet appointment later in the day. Misty however, had other plans. The seven-year
old Saskatoon native decided to take matters into her own paws when things did not go
according to her usual weekly schedule by digging herself out and under her backyard
fence and setting herself free. Thankfully, it’s only about a 10-minute walk to Fifi’s Day
and Play, so once at her destination, Misty’s owner was called, and she was safely
returned.

In response to the burden of increased infrastructural repair costs that Canadians are
facing during this period of atmospheric fragmentation, Prime Minister Carney has
announced a one time “fragment rehabilitation” credit. The credit, which ranges from
$340 to $612, depending on household size, is available to all Canadians, regardless of
property ownership.

Toronto is officially the first Canadian location for the popular seltzer cafe chain, Bublitz.
The shop, which specializes in a wide range of unique botanical flavours of the sugar
free sparkling beverage, opened its first location in Los Angeles in June, and quickly
found virality for its “Wild bergamot and vanilla espresso martini” flavoured seltzer. The
Toronto location is set to open in early November, at the corner of Shaw and Queen.

Paleontologists worldwide are abuzz after a perfectly preserved bison carcass was
discovered in a bog in Wood Buffalo National Park (AB). Preliminary DNA analysis has
dated the animal as almost 12,000 years old, making it one of the oldest recorded
specimens of wild bison in the world. Moreover, the low oxygen conditions allow for
complex testing from the creatures hair, which might offer new insight into how the
creatures that once covered the prairies might have lived.
