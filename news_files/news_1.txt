Investigators are saying they suspect criminal intent in relation to the fire at the
downtown Toronto Marriott last month. While a suspect has not been announced, the
TPD confirmed in a recent press interview that there is evidence correlating the alleged
arson to individuals who protested an Israeli tech conference that was occurring at the
hotel at the time. No further information is currently available.

The Canadian Supreme Court has reached a verdict on a case of domestic violence
where a Ring camera was used as a critical form of evidence. The Ring camera, while
placed inside a neighbour of the accused’s home, faced a window and captured footage
of the accused’s bedroom. This camera captured clear evidence of abuse, which many
have seen as it leaked onto social media sites last month. However, the court ruled the
evidence inadmissible, as it invaded the privacy rights by non-consensually recording in
a private residence. This made the case unsubstantiated, and the court let the accused
walk free. Although the court said in their verdict that they regretted the lack of
consequences for the accused, the potential ramifications the precedent would set for
privacy rights was too high to indict.
