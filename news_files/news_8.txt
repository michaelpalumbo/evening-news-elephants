Paris Fashion Week kicked off on September 27th, showcasing the womenswear
collection for Spring/Summer 28. and is set to wrap up on the sixth. Fashion houses
showcased so far include Acne Studios, Rick Owens, Givenchy, Chanel, and Saint
Laurent. Louis Vuitton will close out the event on the sixth.

NASA has announced it is reinstating all operations, after a seven month hiatus from
any orbital operations. The agency says that, despite the high risk for individual
astronauts, the potential value of discovering the cause of atmospheric instability is too
high for any further delay. Canada, the United States and Russia have all pledged a
doubling of their countries’ respective space research budget, a potentially
unprecedented international collaboration.

The levees around Granville Island in metro Vancouver broke yesterday after a large
atmospheric fragment fell into the Strait of Georgia, sending large waves throughout the
area. The waves reached False Creek around 8PM, and the area has been inaccessible
since. This is the third levee break since their construction last year, further bringing into
question whether the island is still economically viable for the city.

The Girl Group Katseye loses original member Manon Bannerman as she pursues a
solo career. Bannerman announced a hiatus from the group in 2025, but confirmed that
she has officially parted ways with Katseye.

Today marks the one month anniversary of the autonomous zone situated in
Debaltseve, near the contentious border between Ukraine and Russia. The region
declared independence from both nations and a full demilitarization of the area in
September, and continues to operate. While neither Russia or Ukraine support the
zone, both consider members inside their civilians, and neither have been willing to
attack nor negotiate with the zone.
