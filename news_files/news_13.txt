The International Olympic Committee has announced they will not be holding the Winter Olympics in 2030, citing both atmospheric instability and increasing temperatures as causes for the indefinite suspension. While the French Alps, which were designated to host the 2030 games, are not the site of frequent sky impacts, the lack of snow cover during this past summer has indicated the region likely is no longer able to create the ideal conditions for many of the snow-reliant sports. In a press release the Committee stated they have not yet decided whether they will instead have a 2030 summer olympics, or skip the year entirely.
