Toronto Mayor Olivia Chow celebrates her re-election with an “around the world” victory
celebration, with pop-up dance parties scheduled in Little Italy, Little Jamaica, Little
Tibet, Greektown, Koreatown and Chinatown this weekend. In yesterday’s Bloor-Yonge
flash mob, she promised decreased wait times on the TTC, increased library hours, and
another 2.3 for police overtime spending.

Toy retailers are recalling Little Tikes “Babies First DJ Sets” after paint tests have come
back positive for lead. If you purchased the toy set between January and September
2027, please contact your retailer for information about receiving a refund.

Steven Spielberg has just announced the stars of his cinematic retelling of the literary
classic, “Grapes of Wrath.” The film, set to be released in 2029, stars Robert Pattinson
as Tom Joad and Pedro Pascal as Jim Casey. For those who can’t remember 11th
grade English class, Steinbeck’s novel follows the story of a working class farmer
through the dust bowl, exploring themes of exploitation and American idealism. In an
interview Spielberg stated he found the story “startlingly relevant” to our current age.
