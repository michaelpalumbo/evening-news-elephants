Toronto’s Bike Lawyer won a cyclist 1.2 million in a landmark suit against GardaWorld,
after one of their armoured vehicles pulled into the bike lane without signalling, all while
a cyclist was already within the lane. Thankfully, the cyclist, Jerry Goodshaw was a
seasoned rider, and was able to jump up to the sidewalk, managing to get by with only
minor scrapes from the side of the truck. The prosecution argued that without the rider’s
skills, the truck almost certainly would have caused a fatality, and therefore had to pay
to the rider what the payout for such a tragedy would pay to a family. In a statement
after the verdict, Goodshaw urged cyclists to remain vigilant and shouted out local
mutual aid initiative Bike Brigade for pushing him to maintain his cycling skills.
