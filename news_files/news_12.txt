Prisoners at California Correctional Facility have begun a hunger strike after they were
assigned the task of constructing a new, nearby data centre as mandatory labour
designation. Over 1,100 of the prison’s residents have joined in on the strike, refusing to
work on the State’s new data expansion project.

On a lighter note, a purse thief in Dublin was apprehended when a small fragment of
aerial debris struck him in mid stride just after he had ripped the purse away from an
elderly pedestrian. Arriving at the scene police found the woman identified as Siobhan
O’Flanerty, relentlessly pelting her assailant with loose change while shouting
obscenities. The purse thief was taken to St James's Hospital where he was later
discharged and taken to lock-up.

Nova Scotia votes next Monday on a bill to decide whether or not the province will
mandate measles vaccines for infants over the age of 12 months. This controversial bill
makes Nova Scotia the second province to pass such a mandate, after New Brunswick
passed a similar bill last November.
#####
California has finally begun enforcing its water restrictions, issuing a whopping 8,690
fines in its first week of enforcement. While limitations on water usage for lawns and
other personal use has been under restriction since September, enforcement of the
restriction began this past Monday. Fines for recreational water use can reach a
maximum of $15,000 for repeat offenders.
#####
The vaccine for Lyme disease is once again available in Ontario pharmacies, after a
slow ramp up of Canadian production had created a temporary shortage. Most
pharmacies had appointments booked for all available vaccines within hours of opening
up registration, following two seasons of incredibly high tick activity in the province.
Anyone interested in booking a vaccine should contact their local pharmacy for more
information about when their shipments will become available.
