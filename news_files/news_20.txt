Rhinoceros News - The Northern White Rhino is now extinct. The final 2 rhinos in the world resided in the Ol Pejeta Conservancy, Kenya which was struck by a substantial fragment of Firmament earlier today. Though no humans were harmed in the strike, but Suni and Sudan, the last white rhinos in the world were. The white rhino formerly ranged over parts of northwestern Uganda, southern South Sudan, the eastern part of Central African Republic, and northeastern Democratic Republic of the Congo.
