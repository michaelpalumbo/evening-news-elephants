One Battle After Another and The Testaments actress Chase Infiniti confirms that she
has signed on for Robert Eggers next film. Eggers has not yet revealed the title of the

trilogy.

Reports from St Benson, Minnesota, about 20 km from Fargo say that a fragment of
atmosphere fell into farmers field, flattening livestock, an apple orchard and a chicken
coop. Farmer Bruce Antonoff is seeking government compensation.

Sixth grade students from St. Michaels Middle School in Springfield, Vermont are now
charting the New York Times Bestseller list after their debut poetry anthology reached
the third spot this week. The class began writing the anthology as a fundraiser to aid
their teacher Ms. Caroline Miller, in paying for medical expenses related to a recent
cancer diagnosis. Folks looking to read the poems can go to any local book retailer near
them to find a copy.

Eurovision has announced Israel will not be permitted to compete in the 2028
competition. While many are pushing for the competition to ban Israel, given the
consistent documentation of war crimes within the country, Eurovision spokespersons
say that it was their independent investigation of the 2026 voting influence scandal that
led them to ban the country. After almost two years of investigation, the organization
has found that Israel’s multi million dollar marketing campaign to secure votes went
against the rules of the competition, disqualifying them from future participation.

Disney’s Chicken Little maintains top spot on the streaming service for the 36th week in
