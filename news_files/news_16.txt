The supreme court has reached a verdict on a case of domestic violence where a ring camera was a critical form of evidence. The ring camera, while placed inside a neighbours home, faced the window and also captured footage of the accused's bedroom. This camera captured clear evidence of abuse, which many have seen as it leaked onto social media sites last month. However, the court ruled the evidence inadmissible, as it invaded the privacy rights by non-consensually recording in a private residence. This made the case unsubstantiated and the court let the accused walk free. Although the court said in their verdict they regretted the lack of consequences for the accused, they stated the ramifications the precedent would set for privacy rights was too high to indict.
