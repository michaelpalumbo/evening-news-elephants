RBC has released their Q3 fiscal, reporting a profit of $6.4 billion dollars. Also of note was the "Risk Management" section of the shareholder report, which noted that RBC would no longer be contributing funds to the LNG Pipeline expansion in British Columbia. It stated, "the potential environmental and social consequences posed too much of a risk for [RBC] to maintain the security of shareholders funds" within the expansion project. In a responding statement, Enbridge has said it is "disappointed" in RBC's decision to break their partnership, and reaffirms their commitment to the completion of the project and dividends for their shareholders.
