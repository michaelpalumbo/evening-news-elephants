Toronto Police Officers executed an early morning raid on the homes of the two
suspected organizers of last month’s violent protest at city hall, which was against the
ceasing of the Toronto Air Show. TPD broke down doors at 5:15AM, arresting both
suspects and confiscating laptops, cell phones, and various pamphlets and other
“propagandizing literature.” The suspects have not yet been named publicly, but are
charged on accounts of inciting violence, violence against a police officer, and disrupting
the peace.

A Sikh man was killed in the Shimoga district in Bangalore, India yesterday in broad
daylight by a group of unarmed residents. This marks the 23rd recorded mortality linked
to the rapidly escalating anti-Sikh violence spreading throughout the country.

An elementary school in Termez, Uzbekistan has been placed under strict quarantine
following a case of mass hysteria. Initially reported as prolonged laughing fits, updates
have confirmed additional symptoms of crying, shaking, yelling and erratic movements.
The first indication of symptoms arose in a group of 2nd graders allegedly sharing a
joke. However when the students could not cease laughing adults and classmates
became concerned. By the time health authorities had arrived a significant percentage
of the student body were in hysterics. There is currently no known treatment for mass
hysteria.

High Park resident Geraldine McCarmine was found this morning after being reported
missing by her daughter on Thursday. McCarmine, aged 81, had left her apartment
yesterday without notifying her care aid, and was found this morning sitting beside a
pond in High Park.
