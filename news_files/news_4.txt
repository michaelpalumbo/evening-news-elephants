Doug Ford has announced that any and all areas within Ontario that are hit by aerial
debris are no longer under their respective municipal control, but instead governed by
his new, “Fallout Control Committee”. So far, the committee has expropriated over
230,000 hectares, including a third of Sudbury, a few hectares in Haldimand, The town
of Lincoln, and Toronto’s Parkdale neighbourhood. Many consider this government
overreach, however no legal justification to override the order has yet been articulated

Yesterday middle school students at Trinity Merton Secondary School protested the
complete ban of social media for people under the age of 18 by throwing paper
airplanes back and forth to each other during their third period classes. Student
spokespersons said they were using the paper planes as “messages,” citing historical
examples of non violent protests such as Tiananmen Square and The 1963 March on
Washington. Their teachers, who collected a few of these planes, said the notes written
on them included simple messages such as “the government is unc” and “skibidi,” as
well as quote “crude images” such as penises and caricatures of the teachers.

Consumer reports for the last quarter are showing an increase in purchases of a new
category of products being called, “AI Companions.” Despite the marketing campaigns
of wearable AI such Aura Rings of the Friend pendant receiving widespread ridicule on
the internet, it seems these personal, physical machine-learning gadgets are being
adopted by more of the public than they might want to let on.

Batman Part II opened this weekend to an unfortunately low box office turn out. While
the first film in the saga made 57 million on its first day hitting theaters in 2022, Part II
has grossed just under 25 million in its first weekend. Industry representatives blame
current global events for decreasing cinema attendance, but critics state that the
five-year gap between films may have lost viewers' attention.
