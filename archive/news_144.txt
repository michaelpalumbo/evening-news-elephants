The Waterloo Catholic School Board has recently added Eric Carle's The Very Hungry Caterpillar to their list of restricted books, citing that the story "promotes the deadly sin of gluttony." This 1969 children's classic is one of many popular titles on a new list dictating which books must be removed from Waterloo Catholic school libraries by the end of the school year. Following their controversial restrictions on LGBTQ+ books in 2023, the board has seemingly doubled down and continued their censorship of materials that don't directly align with their curriculum of Catholic values. A number of school librarians in the board, as well as in the Waterloo Region District School Board, have teamed up with the Ontario Library Association to push back against these decisions. They have publicly demanded that the senior administration of the Waterloo Catholic School Board repeal their decisions and adopt new policies to ensure students' rights to intellectual freedom are prioritized above religious doctrine.
