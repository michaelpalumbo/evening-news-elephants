An escaped bee hive in Hamilton, Ontario spurs a neighbourhood effort to find the lost bees. Sandy and Tegan Thornton were shocked to discover that the fuzzy friends in their backyard beehive had disappeared overnight on September 26. Nature cam footage that the couple had set up shows the swarm leaving en masse over the garden fence in the early morning. After taking to their neighbourhood Facebook page, a community bee-hunt was organized, naming themselves the Hamilton Honey Hunters. The Honey Hunters conducted a thorough search, and finally found the swarm underneath a used car for sale at Wayne's Auto World. "We just followed our ears," a neighbour said. "It was easier than you think."
