The first annual TD Toronto Festival of Toronto Film (TFsquared) finds its home at the Beaches' Fox Theatre. "It's exciting to showcase this imperfect and challenging city at this historic venue", commented Moss Abu Bakr, the festival's founder. The festival will include a 24-hour film challenge that awards $1000 for the audience favourite Toronto-centred short film produced within the timeframe. The festival runs November 7th-November 9th 2027.
