Doug Ford has announced that any and all areas within Ontario that are hit by aerial debris are no longer under their respective municipal control, but instead governed by his new, "Fallout Control Committee". So far, the committee has expropriated over 230,000 hectares, including a third of Sudbury, a few hectares in Haldimand, The town of Lincoln, and Toronto's Parkdale neighbourhood. Many consider this government overreach, however no legal justification to override the order has yet been articulated
