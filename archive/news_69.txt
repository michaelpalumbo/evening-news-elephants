In Florence repairs continue on the Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore after a falling fragment shattered the north ceiling of the dome yesterday. This World UNESCO Heritage site, completed in 1436, is the 37th Catholic building to be damaged by aerial debris. The disproportionate number of Catholic institutions being damaged vs those of other religions has led Pope Leo XIV to post on X that this is merely an unfortunate coincidence and does in no way reflect the opinions of a higher power. To which he punned in a separate post..."or at least any higher power beyond the sky".
