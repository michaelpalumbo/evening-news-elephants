Japan's bear troubles continue with multiple recent attacks by Asiatic black bears in urban areas such as Ōshū and Kitakami. Experts believe that shrinking populations in rural areas have made it less intimidating for bears to come into urban spaces, and that many have now lost their fear of humans. However this story is not just about nature's Goliaths, but also it's Davids. Pickles, a pure bred white chihuahua, resides with owner Noriko just outside the urban Kitakami. Last Thursday Noriko was taking out garbage to a back alley disposal unit, when she surprised an unsuspecting black bear. As now seen by millions from released closed circuit security footage, the startled bear became aggressive preparing to attack when Pickles came to the rescue. Inserting himself between the bear and his owner; growling and barking and spinning in circles, Pickles held the bear at bay while Noriko and then Pickles escaped to safety. Though there has been some dispute among experts as to if the bear was afraid of Pickles or was simply confused, one thing is for certain... Heroes come in all sizes.
