Scientists at JAXA, the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, will soon begin a study on creating a human outpost on the moon. This follows NASA's plans unveiled in 2026 to build the Moon Base, which would be located permanently at the moon's south pole. The Japanese space agency has no confirmed plans to build an outpost at this time. However, JAXA President Hiroshi Yamakawa expressed his commitment to Japan's involvement in lunar exploration, saying that this study is the natural progression of JAXA's current lunar technology research missions.
