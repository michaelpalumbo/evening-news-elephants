Kronosure has been charged with selling minors' likenesses. The computers of the Toronto-based start up were seized this Tuesday following an anonymous tip that the company stored the images taken for age verification for access through an un-advertized subscription. In turn this subscription allowed the stored images to be incorporated into AI generated videos. Meta denies any knowledge of this service, and has vowed to restructure its subsidiary. Parents argue it's "not enough". A list of subscribers has been subpoenaed from Kronosure.
