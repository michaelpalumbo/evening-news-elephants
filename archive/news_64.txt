Curatorial staff at the Acropolis Museum in Athens were greeted with a surprise this morning as they arrived at work to find almost 5 tonnes of historic marble statue in front of the entrance. The statues have been confirmed to be part of West Pediment, illustrating the Battle of Poseidon and Athena. These fragments had been housed in the British Museum for over 150 years, but had gone missing in June, in one of the most baffling archeological heists of the modern age. While the thieves of these statues are still unknown to Interpol, Greeks around the world are celebrating the return of their artifacts after decades of requests for repatriation, denied by the British Museum.
