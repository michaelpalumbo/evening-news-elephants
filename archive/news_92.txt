The late artist David Lynch continues to break records with the sale of A Man and His Dog. Just weeks after I FIX MY HEAD became the highest selling work by a 21st century artist at Christie's, this week's sale has shattered the record with a whopping $178-million dollar price tag. Lynch's tidal popularity has led to real debate as to how history will understand the primary legacy of his artistic contribution. Will Lynch be remembered as a filmmaker who painted...or a painter who happened to make movies?
