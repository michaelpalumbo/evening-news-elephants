Development of a new luxury condo complex in Bucerias, Mexico (an hour outside of Puerto Vallarta) has been halted after a series of destructive acts of vandalism. These acts are speculated to be caused by local cartels, though no one has come forward to claim the attacks. Since development by real estate company Padre del Casa began in 2014, property values in the area have tripled, while many residents still struggle to pay for groceries. The rapid inflation has been attributed to PDC's exponential growth in the area, which may be creating tensions between locals and tourists.
