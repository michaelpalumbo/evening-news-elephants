Steven Spielberg has just announced the stars of his cinematic retelling of the literary classic, "Grapes of Wrath." The film, set to be released in 2029, stars Robert Pattinson as Tom Joad and Pedro Pascal as Jim Casey. For those who can't remember 11th grade English class, Steinbeck's novel follows the story of a working class farmer through the dust bowl, exploring themes of exploitation and American idealism. In an interview Spielberg stated he found the story "startlingly relevant" to our current age.
