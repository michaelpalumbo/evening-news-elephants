Researchers at Columbia University have shared new findings about the long-term side effects of weight loss medications such as Ozempic and Wegovy. The risks of gastrointestinal issues have been widely recognized for years now, but there has been a recent surge in reports of colon polyps - some of them cancerous - related to long-term use of GLP-1 medications. Doctors across North America have shared their concerns following two fatal cases that have ruled GLP-1 medication-induced colon cancer as the cause of death. The public relations and media departments at Ozempic and Wegovy, respectively, have yet to release statements on the situation.
