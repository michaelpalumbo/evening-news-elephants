Netflix announces a new addition to the streaming platform: Netflix original vertical dramas. Vertical dramas have been increasing in popularity online, and the major streamer is the first to integrate the new media form into the mainstream. Releasing early next year, here are some of the first titles we know about: "The Bodyguard I Shouldn't Fall For," "Getting Revenge on the Playboy," "My Fiancé Doesn't Know I'm Royalty," and "Onyx Pack: Betrayal of the Alpha Wolf."
