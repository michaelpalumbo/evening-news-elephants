The European Union recommends against the buying of coltan on the European continent, begins talks with members to ban its sale. In response to the week of violent protests targeting corporations like Apple and Microsoft following the October 2nd Kinshasa mine collapse that claimed the life of 17 miners, 7 of them under the age of 18, the European Union has recommended against the buying of coltan by European technology corporations. Across the week of protests, 300 people have been arrested, most non-European citizens. In an attempt to calm the public and, according to experts, curb extradition costs, the EU has promised to support a DRC-centralized coltan market to reduce the exploitation of Congolese coltan miners who are not adequately compensated for their dangerous labour by middleman buyers.
