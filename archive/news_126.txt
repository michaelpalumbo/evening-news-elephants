Mayor Brad Bradford vows to increase policing in university areas to curb "ideologically-driven" crime, arguing campuses are "ground zero for anti social extremism". In a recent X post, Bradford wrote, "Our Post Secondary Institutions have strayed from preparing our students for participation in our society, to leading them to instead believe they need to burn it down to start a new one". There is no evidence that the suspects are University of Toronto students or affiliated with any other post secondary institution in Toronto.
