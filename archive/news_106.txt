1 dead and 2 injured in a crash on Highway 401 in Scarborough yesterday night. The crash happened in the westbound express lanes near Kennedy Road shortly after 10:30pm. Police say a 75-year-old man from Windsor was pronounced dead at the scene, and two people were taken to hospital with non-life-threatening injuries. Parts of Highway 401 west were closed for several hours after the incident but have since reopened. Anyone who witnessed the collision is being asked to contact investigators.
