HBO's Euphoria director Sam Levinson announces a new project: an adaptation of Aldous Huxley's 1932 novel Brave New World. This is Levinson's first project since the final season of Euphoria aired in 2026, which struggled against years of delays and scheduling conflicts to ultimately be met with mixed reviews and a 44% rating on Rotten Tomatoes. Levinson is keeping the details close to his chest for now, but has confirmed that it will be a limited series on HBO, as well as told Vanity Fair that it will follow "the spirit of the novel, if not the letter." Known for being a provocateur and embracing the controversial, it is impossible to predict what Levinson's adaptation of the classic will look like.
