Indonesia has announced a new, 29 billion dollar redevelopment plan for Jakarta that they claim will be on the "cutting edge, nature-based design." The project, which is estimated to be finished in 2035, include the daylighting of multiple urban waterways that are currently channelled, and an ambitious plan to bring the city's plant biodiversity up to at least ⅔ of their forest diversity. While the plan is costly, urban planning experts cite the success of other naturalization projects such as the restoration of the Chonggyechon (Chon-gay-chon) in South Korea as strong indicators that the project could be a success.
