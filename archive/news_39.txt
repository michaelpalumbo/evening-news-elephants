Host of successful podcast Call Her Daddy and co-founder of Unwell Beverages, was recently caught in the splash zone. Protestors interrupted Cooper's keynote speech at the Women in Tech conference in Toronto on September 22, spraying water guns full of what guests assumed was a red sports beverage, and calling for Cooper to cease her business partnership with Nestle for her line of Unwell Beverages. After protestors were removed by security, Cooper commented to the crowd that, "This is why it's so hard for women to succeed. We're subjected to so many purity tests, and it's almost always other women who are trying to drag us down."
