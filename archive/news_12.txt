An explosion rang out Friday October 1st at 8:07 pm at Queens Park, drawing onlookers to Queens Park Circle and creating a column of smoke over the east wing of the building. No injuries are reported. A sanitation worker, who requested not to be named, the only individual on site, reports being in the legislature's entrance way upon hearing the blast. They discovered a small fire in the east first floor washrooms, and were able to extinguish it before it spread.
