On a lighter note, a purse thief in Dublin was apprehended when a small fragment of aerial debris struck him in mid stride just after he had ripped the purse away from an elderly pedestrian. Arriving at the scene police found the woman identified as Siobhan O'Flanerty, relentlessly pelting her assailant with loose change while shouting obscenities. The purse thief was taken to St James's Hospital where he was later discharged and taken to lock-up.
