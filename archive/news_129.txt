Landmark case rules "special treatment" given to LGBTQ+ individuals discriminatory. Resolved earlier this week in the Supreme Court, the case of Bondille vs TSMOOTHIE has awarded Marina Bondille $5000 in damages to be paid by TSMOOTHIE, a LGBTQ+-centric smoothie shop and art space that "exists to provide trans people with a meaningful and flexible way to support their livelihoods". Bondille contended she was charged double for her smoothie compared to the person in front of her, who she perceived as a member of the LGBTQ+ community. While Bondille did pay the advertised price, the Supreme Court has ruled against identity-based discount policies and has found TSMOOTHIE at fault. Critics are keen to see if this ban will extend to industry-specific reciprocal agreements and veterans discounts.
