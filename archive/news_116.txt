Canada has announced it will not be following suit of other countries that have created a new asylum category for refugees fleeing areas impacted by atmospheric fragmentation. Thus far, Sweden, Iceland, Denmark, the Netherlands and Australia have all created pathways to accept additional refugee claimants, with many imagining Canada to be the next country to follow suit, given its vast area and relatively low frequency of impacts. However, Prime Minister Carney stated in a press release this morning that while the country "regretted not being able to welcome fragmentation refugees with open arms, the nation had to prioritize its own stability in this tumultuous time"
