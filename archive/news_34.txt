In the wake of the release of the Joni Mitchell biopic, several A-listers reveal that they were in the running for the highly-coveted role. While Meryl Streep and Anya-Taylor Joy gave performances that garnered plenty of critical acclaim, audiences are now wondering what Cameron Crowe's film would have been like if different actors had stepped into the part. While we can hardly call these celebs 'unsuccessful,' here are some of the stars who were passed over for the part: Sydney Sweeney, Scarlett Johansson, Diana Agron, Dove Cameron, Sabrina Carpenter, Alicia Silverstone, Zara Larsson, Glenn Close, Kate Hudson, and Charlize Theron. If you've been following this film since before it was even officially announced, you may notice that one dulcimer-strumming starlet is noticeably absent. Amanda Seyfried, who sang Mitchell's song 'California' on Jimmy Fallon in March of 2025, revealed that she was attached to a previous Joni Mitchell biopic developed in 2020, which ultimately fell through. In regards to Crowe's project, Seyfried said, "What I know about that project is that I'm very, very much aged-out of [playing] young Joni." Considering the age range of the women who tried to play the Canadian-born songstress, maybe Amanda would have been justified in shooting her shot!
