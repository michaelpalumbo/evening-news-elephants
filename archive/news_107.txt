A 16-year old has been arrested in connection with the recent acts of graffiti that have been occurring at Bloor-Yonge station. Over the past three weeks, ads promoting a new proposed nuclear facility in Pickering have been consistently vandalized by a masked graffiti artist, whose identity had befuddled TPD until they were caught in the act late Thursday evening. The identity of the accused has not been released, due to their age, however police were pleased to report the reduction in vandalism at the station.
