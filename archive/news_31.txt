Toronto Zoo welcomes a new red panda cub with a call to the public to vote on her name. Since opening their new red panda habitat in the summer of 2026, Toronto Zoo says it is "a great success to have a breeding pair thrive the way that Poppy and Kalden are, and we hope it will be the same for their new cub." Visitors won't be able to see the new cub for some time, however, as mom and baby will be spending this delicate period in the specially designed maternity dens. While cub survival rates are low, the Zoo remains optimistic about the supportive environment that the new habitat offers.
