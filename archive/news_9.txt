Continued investigations into the disruption to Pope Leo XIV's Golden Jubilee last month reveal that the robot dogs released during the event were all run under a single-origin software, called Ubisan. Ubisan is an open source software created collectively online by AI enthusiasts, and is available for free to anyone who has the skills to use it. Given this software ambiguity, it is unlikely investigators will be able to use the program's digital footprint to discover who initiated the attack. Interpol has stated it is working with cutting-edge tech experts to apprehend the culprit, and are confident that their team will be able to bring them to justice.
