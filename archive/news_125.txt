A collective of private funders has come together to combat Ford's OSAP cuts. TD, RBC, CIBC, and BMO have created the Ontario Post Secondary Funders Association (OPSFA) as a reaction to student discontent over the Ford government's OSAP cuts. When asked for comment, a TD spokesperson replied, "We are committed to supporting the next generation of Canadian visionaries, whether it's through larger grants to institutions or supporting students directly. Joining OPFSA was a natural step in our commitment to investing in Ontario's future." While many students rejoice to learn that they will not be inevitably saddled with major debt, critics worry that this increased involvement of banking institutions with the livelihood of the general Canadian public will continue to shift the balance of political power towards the wills of these economic powerhouses.
