Recent investigation by independent journalists has uncovered that multiple popular brands of e-bikes including, CityRide, DCBike and Cloverdale have begun including tracking sensors within the bikes frames, without any indication or opt-out option for consumers. The investigation has tied the collected data to multiple third-party data collection and sale platforms, indicating that the e-bike companies likely are profiting off the data. CityRide refused to comment, and Cloverdale released a statement saying that, quote, "We are currently conducting an internal investigation, to see if there is a potential leak in data from our facility. Cloverdale does collect data from its riders, but only for the purpose of functionality and repair work. This data is anonymized, and holds no identifying factors that could connect the data to the individual e-bike holder. While we at Cloverdale are concerned at the possibility that this data may have made it outside our organization, rest assured, all collected data is anonymized thoroughly, through our own randomizing software."
