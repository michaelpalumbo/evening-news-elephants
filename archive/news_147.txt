Elon Musk has purchased Pinterest for $14 billion. The acquisition comes after months of Musk tweeting at Pinterest developpers on his platform X, formerly known as Twitter, urging them to incorporate his AI bot, Grok, into their platform. It seems as though the incessant tweeting has proved successful, and the Pinterest team has agreed to hand over the reins. In a statement posted on all official Pinterest social media accounts, the company shares: "We are thrilled to see what new ventures Elon and Grok will bring to Pinterest. We believe there's no one better to carry on our mission to bring everyone the inspiration to create a life they love." Much like his renaming of Twitter to X, Musk has announced that he will be rebranding the popular digital pinboard app. He put out a poll to his loyal cult following so they can weigh in on the new name, with the leading options so far including PinX and X.2. For a platform based on collecting inspiration, let's hope that the new Chief Technology Officer can get a bit more inspired himself when it comes to the reinvention of this long-beloved site.
