Misty the golden retriever/husky mix has made news by taking herself to doggie daycare Friday morning. Misty's owner had kept her home this past Friday because she had a vet appointment later in the day. Misty however, had other plans. The seven-year old Saskatoon native decided to take matters into her own paws when things did not go according to her usual weekly schedule by digging herself out and under her backyard fence and setting herself free. Thankfully, it's only about a 10-minute walk to Fifi's Day and Play, so once at her destination, Misty's owner was called, and she was safely returned.
