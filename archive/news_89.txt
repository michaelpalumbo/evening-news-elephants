With the recent ceasefire in the middle east, Shoshana Bedrosian Press secretary for the Likud government has verified that the Iron Dome (Israel's missile defense system) is being recalibrated to defend against fragments of the decaying firmament. Critics have already stated concerns that the use of intercepting missiles will result in altering trajectories of fragments creating safety concerns for neighboring nations. The Netanyahu government has already commented that any adjacent collateral damage is simply an unintended consequence of Israeli security.
